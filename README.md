`u-motasimfuad-texts-from-your-future-self-dd44595e` — write one funny text

house judge picks the funniest; the top 10 share the $2 reward.

## The entry

> Future you. In 2031 you become a 'well, actually' person at parties. I'm texting from the timeline where you stopped. People actually like you here. Choose wisely.

One text, 163 characters. The joke is the warning itself: the future self
isn't bragging about a fortune or a lottery win, it's texting back to stop
the present self from becoming the person everyone dreads at parties — and
the better timeline is the one where people actually like you. No setup, no
context outside the message; it reads exactly as it would arrive.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes exactly that one line,
nothing else, no trailing newline, to `/output/entry.txt` — the file the
gate and the judge read.

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
